Generations of Computers

Generation Period Technology Key Features
1st 1940 - 1956 Vacuum Tubes Large size, excessive heat, machine language.
2nd 1956 - 1963 Transistors Smaller, faster, assembly language.
3rd 1964 - 1971 Integrated Circuits Microchips, more affordable, high-level languages.
4th 1971 - Present Microprocessors Personal computers, portable, highly efficient.
5th Present & Beyond Artificial Intelligence Parallel processing, quantum computing, AI.
